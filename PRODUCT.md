# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

GRC practitioners reading practical AI workflows and perspectives from club writers.

## Product Purpose

The GRC Engineering Club AI Blog shares hands-on ways to use AI in governance, risk, and compliance work, with room for AI governance articles.

## Brand Personality

Warm, practical, curious. A calm reading space inspired by Catppuccin peach, connected to the club without its bright orange and dark theme.

## Anti-references

Avoid bright orange marketing treatments and the previous lime-and-mint workbench aesthetic.

## Design Principles

- Lead with useful, practical writing.
- Give different writers room for their perspectives.
- Keep the club connection while expressing a softer identity.
- Make long articles easy to read on any screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep text contrast readable, keyboard focus visible, and respect reduced motion preferences.
